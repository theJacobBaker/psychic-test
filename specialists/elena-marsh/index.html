About Elena Marsh

Elena Marsh has spent nineteen years in conversation with people about the one subject that makes everyone a beginner: love. Her listing on Psychic Test reads Love & Relationships, but she describes the work more simply. People bring her a situation, she listens for the sentence they keep avoiding, and together they put that sentence on the table where it can be looked at calmly.

She came to this work through years of informal readings for friends, and she still favours a plain, kitchen-table manner over theatre. Elena works mainly with the senses people describe in the clairsentient range: an impression in the body, a mood that does not match the words, the odd certainty that a conversation is not finished. She treats those impressions as material to reflect on, never as proof about what another person feels or will do. Anyone who tells you otherwise is guessing, and she says so.

The questions she meets most often are about contact and distance: should I reach out, why did it go quiet, is this person ready for what I want. She also spends a surprising amount of time with people who are not in a crisis at all, only tired of repeating one pattern in new places. Her habit is to ask what you would do if no answer were coming, because the honest reply to that usually shows what the decision really is. She does not promise reunions, and she will not predict that a partner will change.

A reading with Elena is paced slowly. She will often stop to restate what she has heard in her own words, and she invites correction. Expect a handful of themes rather than a long list of predictions, a few questions to take away, and a clear line between what she senses and what is yours to decide. Readings are offered for reflection and entertainment, not as therapy or advice on health, money or law, and she will say when a topic belongs with a licensed professional instead.

If you are not sure whether love is even the right topic, the tests below are a gentle way in. Several of them were written with her focus in mind, and her own work often starts from the same place: noticing how you take in other people's feelings before deciding what to do about them.

How a conversation with Elena goes

  1. Name the situation

    You describe it in a few sentences, in your own words, with nothing left to impress her.

  2. Listen for the avoided sentence

    She reflects back the line you keep stepping around.

  3. Offer a few themes

    Impressions are framed as possibilities to test against your own life.

  4. Leave you the next step

    You finish with one question and one small action, chosen by you.

Questions Elena likes to work with

  • Should I reach out first, or let the silence do its work?
  • Why do I keep ending up in the same kind of relationship?
  • How do I tell the difference between my feelings and theirs?
  • Is it kinder to say it plainly or to let it fade?
  • What am I actually hoping to hear?

Elena: frequently asked questions

Does Elena predict whether someone will come back?

No. She reflects patterns, feelings and choices that are in front of you. She does not make promises about what another person will do.

What can I expect in a first reading?

A calm conversation about one situation, a few themes to consider and a question or two to take away. You set the pace and can stop at any point.

Do I need to take a test first?

No. The tests on this site are optional and free. Some people like to arrive with a result to talk through; others simply start with their question.

Is this a replacement for counselling?

No. Readings are for reflection and entertainment. If a relationship involves safety concerns or you are struggling, please contact a licensed professional or local support service.
